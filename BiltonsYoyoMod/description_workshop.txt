* Update Log : (v0.93 - yoyos only)
Major Revamp (Yoyo Revamp)

-Pre-Hardmode:
Eye Yoyo : Reworked into an occasional dash toward nearby enemies.
Corruption Yoyo : Improved distance based trails, afterimages, and spike behavior.
Bee Yoyo : Fixed honey radius and projectile tracking issues.
Crimson Yoyo : Clone spawning is now based on HP instead of hits.
Deer Yoyo : Improved radius visuals and fade animations.
Skeletron Yoyo : Bones now smoothly chase and track enemies.
Royal Yoyo : Vertical pulls can trigger a powerful double damage attack.
Flesh Yoyo : Improved minion attachment and patterned laser behavior.

-Hardmode:
Destroyer Yoyo : Reworked into a connected 10 clone worm/orbit system with chasing and lasers.
Twins Yoyo : Retinazer and Spazmatism now alternate as the main yoyo with unique attacks.
Prime Yoyo : Replaced the old hands with four specialized Prime weapons.
Hallow Yoyo : Added three orbiting slimes that attack enemies when the player is hit.
Duke Yoyo : Replaced the old wave attack with timed, fading vortex projectiles.
Lunatic Yoyo : Added a 10-second cycle between lightning, ice, and clone attacks.
Solar Yoyo : Added random solar projectiles and enemy tracking companions.
Golem Yoyo : Reworked hands, added a defensive core, lasers, bouncing projectiles, and counterattacks.
Plantera Yoyo : Added aggressive, oscillating eaters that can latch onto enemies.
Empress Yoyo : Completely reworked with a lot of new attacks, day/night attack patterns, and HP based phases.

-Endgame:
Lord Yoyo : Completely reworked with new attacks, phases, projectiles, and visual effects.

-Accessory:
Yoyo Adrenaline : Reworked into a 20-hit charge system that grants 1.5x yoyo damage for 5 seconds, with a new visual charge/timer bar.
Yoyo Sub-Emblem / Golden Emblem : Redesigned to better match Terraria's emblem style.

Note : There are still some bugs, balancing, and polishing needed
